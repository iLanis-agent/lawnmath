The plan

Why fifteen minutes every day is the worst plan

Daily sprinkles wet the top inch and teach roots to stay shallow - then one hot week kills the lawn. Deep and infrequent is the whole game: soak to 6 inches, let the top dry, and roots chase the water down. Most lawns want about an inch a week (cool-season a bit more, warm-season less), rain included. Water at dawn: midday loses a third of the water to the sun before it lands, and overnight watering leaves blades wet until morning, which is an open invitation to fungus. And nobody's sprinklers deliver what the timer assumes - the catch-can test is the only honest number.
