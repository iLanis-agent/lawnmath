LawnMath

Fifteen minutes every day is the worst way to water a lawn. Here is the plan your grass actually wants.

The timer lie

Nobody's sprinklers deliver what the timer assumes. Five cans and one test run tell you the real precipitation rate - and whether your heads water evenly at all.

The deep truth

Daily sprinkles train shallow roots that die in the first hot week. Deep and infrequent - about an inch a week, rain included, split into a few soakings - grows roots that survive August.

The clock truth

Midday watering loses a third of the water to the sun; overnight invites fungus. Dawn is the window. Clay soil adds one more rule: cycle-and-soak, or the water just rents itself to the street.
